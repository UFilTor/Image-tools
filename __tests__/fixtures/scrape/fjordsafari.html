Be on Time for Your Adventure!

Our boat tours and hikes run on a strict schedule to give you the best experience and keep everyone safe.
Please plan ahead so you have plenty of time to park, use the restroom, go through our check-in procedure, dress up in our safety gear, and most importantly - begin your adventure stress-free.


FjordSafari tours: meet-up is 25min before departure. If late - entry closes 10min prior to departure!
Hikes & customized tours: meet-up is 15min before the departure.

Arriving late can mean missing the safety briefing or even the tour itself!
So please be on time – the mountains & the fjords are ready for you!
